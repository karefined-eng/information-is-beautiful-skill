### Law 2: Honest Scales
> *"Reserve length/position for precise judgment, area for magnitude impressions, angle/colour only for rough grouping. Quantitative axes start at zero (length encodings) — or explicitly break + say so. Area encodings: value is proportional to area, never to radius or width alone. Units are named everywhere they appear."*
